# Property content and verification

Property facts live in lib/site-data.ts, rather than being repeated throughout pages. The source for the current guest count, bedroom and bathroom summary, private ground-floor access, host name, review count, amenities and neighbourhood references is the official [Airbnb listing](https://www.airbnb.co.in/rooms/1590674098743485986), reviewed on 26 October 2026. Instagram is used as the source for the names of the residence’s spaces.

The site presents the residence as one private accommodation: the ground-floor guest portion of a bungalow. The Sun Room and Sky Room are described as bedrooms within that stay, not independent hotel rooms.

The exact street address is intentionally not shown. The neighbourhood guide and map searches use public area names only.

Review rating and count are editable in PROPERTY. Update them only from the live listing and keep listingVerifiedAt current. Do not add guest quotations without approved text and permission.

The owner should confirm details that can change: maximum occupancy, bathroom count, accessibility, Wi-Fi, parking, kitchen rules, paid services, check-in/check-out, cancellation and all house policies. Current booking terms and live availability remain on Airbnb.
